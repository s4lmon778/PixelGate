## Magic UI — Animated Beam

The connection beam in `src/DeviceConnection.tsx` adapts the layered SVG path and
transparent gradient pattern from Magic UI’s Animated Beam. PixelGate uses a
fixed SVG and CSS animation rather than the original Motion/ResizeObserver
implementation. Green styling, transfer state and reduced-motion behavior are
provided by PixelGate.

Source: https://github.com/magicuidesign/magicui/blob/85f97fd27b053339af74ba9dd4ca428ee25989fb/apps/www/registry/magicui/animated-beam.tsx
