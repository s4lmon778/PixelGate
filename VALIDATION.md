## Required physical-device and capacity validation

A **5 GB manifest is not a 5 GB byte transfer**. A 10,000-path manifest test is not a completed 10,000-file media session. Automated tests do not certify 100 GB reliability or phone hardware.

On each target computer, phone, or tablet, record OS/browser versions and first check OPFS worker writes plus the available saving mode. Include older or storage-constrained devices. Test documents, archives, audio, JPEG/HEIC/PNG images, MOV/MP4 video, embedded metadata, nested/Unicode folders, real files over 4 GB, and realistic large queues. Independently hash source and exported destination bytes outside the app.

Exercise screen lock, backgrounding, Wi-Fi loss, refresh and reselection, real storage exhaustion, denied/revoked folder access, exports and duplicate conflicts. Use realistic batches for 100 GB sessions and record quota, free space, elapsed time, throughput, failures, and independent hashes. First-generation Pixel storage may not accommodate 100 GB at once.

Photo-library imports and backup services require separate validation: confirm file visibility, relevant metadata, playback or opening, sync configuration, and completed backup in the chosen app or service. For example, Google Photos testing should include capture dates, GPS where present, video playback, and folder backup configuration. Live Photo recognition and Apple original-resource retrieval are outside the browser guarantee.
